Starter kit

You need less than you think. Here is how to match a hook to your yarn, plus tips and size tables for your first projects.

Pins and markers

Two kinds of pins show up in crochet. Stitch markers (small clips, rings or even safety pins) mark the first stitch of a round or a spot you need to find again, and are the first extra worth buying. Blocking T-pins hold a finished piece flat on a mat while it dries to the right size, which matters most for garments, lace and granny squares.

Match a hook to your yarn

Beginner tips

  • Start with worsted weight (labelled 4 or medium) and a hook between 5.0 and 5.5 mm.
  • Pick one plain, light colour for your first few weeks so every stitch is easy to see.
  • Skip fluffy mohair and novelty yarn until you can spot your stitches by touch.
  • Use a safety pin or hair clip as a stitch marker until you buy a pack.
  • Leave safety eyes and stuffing for your first amigurumi.
  • Fabric feels stiff? Go up a hook size. Gaps and holes? Go down one.

Yarn weights and hook ranges
